Office, file, security, data science and network tools that run completely in the browser. **Every application is a single HTML file**: download it, open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, done. No installation, no administrator rights, no server, no internet connection needed. It also works on thin clients with only a browser, and on Windows, Linux and Android.

## What's new since 1.3.0

**Image editor**
- **Soft selections**: pixels can be selected partly – **feather, grow, shrink, border** – and every tool, filter, copy and move works through them; **quick mask** (`Shift+Q`) to paint the selection with white, black and grey.
- **Brush tips**: soft, square, star, calligraphy, scatter and more, with hardness, spacing, angle and pen pressure; own tips from GIMP brushes (`.gbr`, `.vbr`) and pictures.
- **Text layers** that stay editable; **layer masks** (also those of Photoshop files); **free transform** (`Ctrl+T`: scale, rotate, move, flip).
- **Rotate by any angle**, **straighten**, a **crop tool** (`C`) with aspect ratios.
- New adjustments with live preview: **colour balance, vibrance, threshold, posterize, unsharp mask, median, vignette**.
- Photos open **upright** (EXIF orientation); **Image information** shows camera, date and location and saves a **copy without metadata** (the image itself unchanged).
- Save **GIF** and **ICO**; export the **selection or a layer**; **batch processing** of many images into a ZIP (resize, rotate, watermark, format, names).

**Audio editor**
- **Loudness** in LUFS (EBU R 128): measure and normalise, with a peak ceiling; **truncate silence**; **noise gate**.
- **Click removal**, **de-esser**, **parametric equaliser** with a response curve, **auto duck** (music under a voice, as editable volume automation).
- **Frequency analysis** (spectrum with note names, CSV export).
- **Tags** (title, artist, album, …) in WAV, MP3, FLAC, Ogg and M4A; export **one file per label or per clip** as a ZIP.

**Video editor**
- **Picture effects** (blur, grayscale, sepia, hue, invert) and **green screen** (chroma key).
- **Keyframes** for position, size, opacity and rotation; **freeze frame**; **markers**.
- **Cross-fades** between neighbouring clips (picture and sound), also for a whole track; **normalise** a clip's sound.
- Export a **range**, **only the sound**, or an **animated GIF**; save a project **with its media as a ZIP**.
- **Edit in audio editor**: the sound of a clip goes to the audio editor and comes back when saved there.

## Good to know

- **Small and medium files:** the applications keep their data in the browser's memory, so what they can handle is limited by the memory of one browser tab. They are made for everyday files, not for big data.
- **Saving:** in Chrome and Edge, saving writes directly to the opened file. Firefox downloads a new copy, which you then use to replace the old file.
- **Office files:** charts, cropped pictures and videos are kept; charts are written as pictures with their data (not as native Office charts). Keep the original file until you have checked the result.
- **Network functions** (SSH, FTP/SFTP, e-mail, PostgreSQL, network from Python) need the small **relay** program on your computer, because browsers cannot open such connections themselves. It runs without installation or administrator rights: [`relay/python/relay.py`](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.4.0/relay/python/relay.py) (Python) or [`relay/powershell/relay.ps1`](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.4.0/relay/powershell/relay.ps1) (PowerShell). All other functions work without it.
- **Settings** are stored per origin: applications opened as files and applications served by the relay keep separate settings (move them with **⚙ Settings** → Export / Import, or with a workspace).
- **Help:** press `F1` in any application. Documentation: [user guide](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.4.0/docs/en/user-guide.md), [Benutzerhandbuch](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.4.0/docs/de/benutzerhandbuch.md).
