Office, file, security, data science and network tools that run completely in the browser. **Every application is a single HTML file**: download it, open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, done. No installation, no administrator rights, no server, no internet connection needed. It also works on thin clients with only a browser, and on Windows, Linux and Android.

## What's new since 1.2.0

**Office**
- **Charts** in the spreadsheet and the presentation editor: column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut and scatter charts with title, legend, stacking and values, edited in a dialog with a data grid and a live preview. In the spreadsheet a chart comes from the selected table and can be updated from its cells. Charts of Excel, LibreOffice and PowerPoint files are shown with their data. A chart is saved as a picture that carries its data: Office shows the picture, the suite edits it again.
- **Fonts included:** Arimo, Tinos and Cousine (also as Liberation Sans, Serif and Mono), Carlito, Caladea, Gelasio, Inter, Open Sans, Roboto, Lato, Montserrat, Source Serif 4, Merriweather, JetBrains Mono, Fira Code and Source Code Pro – documents look the same on every system. Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman and Courier New fall back to metric-compatible fonts where they are missing, so pages break as in the original. **Import more fonts** (TTF, OTF, WOFF, WOFF2, also from archives); they are available in all applications and travel with exported settings and workspaces.
- **Document editor:** **comments** with replies, resolve, edit and delete (`Ctrl+Alt+M`), kept in Word and OpenDocument files (also LibreOffice's and Word's replies); insert and edit **links** (`Ctrl+K`); **headers, footers and page numbers**; a **page setup** dialog (paper size, orientation, margins, no pages); **date and time**, symbols and emoji; **save as Markdown**.
- **Spreadsheet:** **print / PDF** (`Ctrl+P`: selection, sheet or all sheets, fit to width or page, repeated header rows, gridlines); **hyperlinks**; **find and replace** in all sheets; **row and column groups**; **notes on cells** (also those of Excel files); **remove duplicates** and **text to columns**.
- **Presentation editor:**
  - **OpenDocument presentations** (`.odp`) – open and save, also with LibreOffice's placeholders and styles.
  - **Select several elements**, align, distribute and **group** them (kept in PowerPoint files).
  - **Bulleted and numbered lists** with levels (`Tab`, `Shift+Tab`).
  - New slides with **built-in layouts**; **video and audio** on slides (played in the show); **cropping** pictures.
  - **Header & footer** with slide numbers and date; **hidden slides**; in the show a **pen, laser pointer, black and white screen**; the **spelling** panel (`F7`).

**All applications**
- **Settings export and import** as JSON or as an **encrypted KeePass database**, choosing what to include – general settings, the application's (in the overview: all applications'), connections and passwords, the relay sign-in, imported fonts. Credentials in an unencrypted file need a confirmation.
- **Archive of the open files:** `F1` → **📦 Open files** puts the open files – with unsaved changes, in the overview a folder per application – into a ZIP that opens in the archive manager.
- **Workspaces** (`.suite.zip`): save the open files and, if you like, the settings, and continue elsewhere; the overview reopens every application in its tab. Credentials are only stored encrypted. An example workspace is in `examples.zip`.

**Fixes**
- On phones, taps on the presentation editor's panel were sometimes ignored after tapping a selected element.
- On phones, opening or closing the presentation editor's format panel no longer ends the text editing, so list and format buttons apply to the selected text.
- Questions asked from the about dialog appear in front of it.
- Several test flakes fixed.

## Good to know

- **Small and medium files:** the applications keep their data in the browser's memory, so what they can handle is limited by the memory of one browser tab. They are made for everyday files, not for big data.
- **Saving:** in Chrome and Edge, saving writes directly to the opened file. Firefox downloads a new copy, which you then use to replace the old file.
- **Office files:** charts, cropped pictures and videos are kept; charts are written as pictures with their data (not as native Office charts). Keep the original file until you have checked the result.
- **Network functions** (SSH, FTP/SFTP, e-mail, PostgreSQL, network from Python) need the small **relay** program on your computer, because browsers cannot open such connections themselves. It runs without installation or administrator rights: [`relay/python/relay.py`](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.3.0/relay/python/relay.py) (Python) or [`relay/powershell/relay.ps1`](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.3.0/relay/powershell/relay.ps1) (PowerShell). All other functions work without it.
- **Settings** are stored per origin: applications opened as files and applications served by the relay keep separate settings (move them with **⚙ Settings** → Export / Import, or with a workspace).
- **Help:** press `F1` in any application. Documentation: [user guide](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.3.0/docs/en/user-guide.md), [Benutzerhandbuch](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.3.0/docs/de/benutzerhandbuch.md).

## Deutsch

Büro-, Datei-, Sicherheits-, Data-Science- und Netzwerkwerkzeuge, die vollständig im Browser laufen. **Jede Anwendung ist eine einzelne HTML-Datei**: herunterladen, in Chrome, Edge oder Firefox öffnen, fertig. **`overview.html`** enthält alle Anwendungen in einer Datei; die einzelnen Dateien sind in der Tabelle oben aufgeführt, **`examples.zip`** enthält Beispieldateien und einen Beispiel-Arbeitsbereich.

**Neu seit 1.2.0:** Diagramme in Tabellenkalkulation und Präsentation (auch aus Excel-, LibreOffice- und PowerPoint-Dateien). Mitgelieferte freie Schriften (u. a. Liberation, Carlito, Caladea, Inter, Roboto, JetBrains Mono) und metrisch gleiche Ersatzschriften für Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman und Courier New; weitere Schriften importieren. Textverarbeitung: Kommentare, Links, Kopf- und Fußzeilen mit Seitenzahlen, Seite einrichten, Datum und Uhrzeit, Speichern als Markdown. Tabellenkalkulation: Drucken / PDF, Hyperlinks, Suchen und Ersetzen, Gruppieren, Notizen, Duplikate entfernen, Text in Spalten. Präsentation: OpenDocument (`.odp`), mehrere Elemente markieren, ausrichten, verteilen und gruppieren, Listen, Layouts, Video und Audio, Bilder zuschneiden, Kopf- und Fußzeile, ausgeblendete Folien, Stift und Laserpointer in der Vorführung, Rechtschreibung. In allen Anwendungen: Einstellungen als JSON oder verschlüsselte KeePass-Datenbank exportieren und importieren (mit Auswahl, Warnung vor unverschlüsselten Zugangsdaten), Archiv aus den geöffneten Dateien, Arbeitsbereiche (`.suite.zip`).

Die Netzwerkfunktionen brauchen das kleine Hilfsprogramm **Relay** (siehe oben). Hilfe gibt es mit `F1` in jeder Anwendung und im [Benutzerhandbuch](https://github.com/AdrianFuchsData/Portable-Web-App-Suite/blob/v1.3.0/docs/de/benutzerhandbuch.md).
